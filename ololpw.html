WEB PROJECT

Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Church

Project Link: Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Church

Made With: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON

Customer

Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Church is located in Port Wentworth, Georgia. When I noticed their website was significantly outdated and lacked modern features like responsive design, I contacted them and offered a refresh. I attended this parish as a child and I had a personal connection to it.

Development

The site initially shared a similar structure to my other parish projects but became more distinct over time as I made changes. In 2026, it received a full revision. Most of the parish's communication with parishioners happens through Facebook, so the site could be streamlined into something easier to maintain. I focused on adding functionality including a toggleable dark mode and an English/Spanish language toggle. This is especially important, as the majority of parishioners are native Spanish speakers.
